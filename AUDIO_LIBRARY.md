# ZaynClock Original Sound Library

This release includes locally hosted, original audio created specifically for ZaynClock:

- Classic telephone ring
- Digital computer ring
- Classic chime
- Desk bell
- Rain on a window
- Ocean shore
- Jungle morning
- Peaceful piano
- Soft guitar
- Acoustic sunrise

No third-party music or recordings are bundled. This avoids attribution uncertainty, external CDN failures, and copyright-claim risk. Ambient and music previews stop automatically after 12 seconds in Settings. Alarm playback loops until the user dismisses or snoozes the alarm.
