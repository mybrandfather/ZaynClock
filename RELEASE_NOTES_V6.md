# ZaynClock v6 – Complete Time & Calendar Release

## Added
- Original Bouncy Blocks clock style.
- Original Modern Analog clock style.
- Sun & Moon Today page with sunrise, sunset, solar noon, day length, moon phase and illumination.
- Islamic Calendar with Hijri date, monthly view, Gregorian dates and ±1 day moon-sighting adjustment.
- Worldwide Holidays page with country/year selection and major national holidays.
- New pages added to Tools, footer and sitemap.

## Fixed and retained
- Physical chess-clock turn switching, increment, pause/resume and deadline-based timing.
- Locally hosted original sound library.
- Footer logo now uses the same reliable local asset as the header.
- Canonical sitemap, robots, legal pages, redirects and existing tool routes retained.

## Important limits
- Holiday data is a starter set of major nationwide dates; regional and lunar holidays require official yearly verification.
- Sun and moon times are estimates and can vary by horizon/elevation.
- Hijri dates can vary by local moon sighting.
- The sprite-GIF clock supplied by the user was not included because it depended on externally hosted CodePen assets with unverified reuse rights. The two new clock styles are original local implementations.
